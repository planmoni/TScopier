1. Executive summary (plain English)

A user who linked her Telegram account on the morning of September 8 was able to copy signals normally for about five hours. Then Telegram suddenly stopped recognising her login (a background security key was revoked), so her account went offline. When the system tried to reconnect her, our worker software on production got stuck — it kept trying but could never reconnect, and it could not be cleanly stopped either. From then on, every attempt she made to reconnect — clicking “Reconnect”, requesting a new code, or using the QR code — timed out and failed.

Nothing she did caused this. The software bug that trapped her was already known and had a written fix, but that fix was not yet running on production. About a day later the stuck background process cleared itself on its own, releasing her account. She is able to connect again with a fresh login. The permanent fix is still waiting to be deployed to production.

2. Issue encountered

  • User simofiore16321@gmail.com reported she could not connect (reconnect) her Telegram account.
  • Investigation of production Railway logs for her user id (8491f3f9) and the prod DB showed the full chain: a clean link, then a Telegram-side key revocation, then a listener that flapped “disconnected but renewing lease anyway” every ~20 seconds for over 20 hours, during which every reconnect or new-login request timed out after ~90 seconds (AUTH_OPERATION_TIMEOUT).

4. Root cause

The trigger (specific to this user): At 2026-09-08 10:58:27 UTC Telegram started rejecting every request from her session with 401 AUTH_KEY_UNREGISTERED — Telegram had revoked/deactivated her auth key. This was isolated to her at that moment (not a multi-user wave), and no duplicate session was detected from our side or any other worker, so it was a Telegram-side key revocation.

Why she could not recover (the bug): The production worker was running a pre-fix build (deployment 0a560378, built 2026-09-04). Two known bugs in that build — the same family as the 2026-09-07 reconnect-storm incident — meant a listener whose key had died could never heal or be stopped:

  • sessionManager.ts — the disconnectedRenewTicks counter (the escape hatch that hard-restarts a stuck listener after ~60s) was deleted every renewal tick, so it never accumulated and the restart never fired (Bug A).
  • userListener.ts — forceReconnect’s client.connect() had no timeout, and stop() awaited the in-flight reconnect forever, so reconnect and stop both wedged; the lease was renewed forever, blocking any other worker from taking the user over (Bug B).

5. The fix

  • The code fix for both bugs already exists on the staging branch (commit 1ecb9e47, 2026-09-08): the heal counter now accumulates across disconnected ticks, and every connect/disconnect/stop step is wrapped in a timeout (withTelegramTimeout / TELEGRAM_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS) so nothing can wedge forever.
  • It has not been merged/deployed to production, which is why this user hit it.
